How the tree was worked out

  • Tomas and Hans are brothers, and Nairal is their uncle, so a sibling of Nairal's must be their parent. (Tel's first handout called them cousins; the table corrected it.)
  • Kostas calls Tomas "another uncle", and Nairal is his great-uncle, so Merrick is drawn as Tomas and Hans's brother. That's a guess; the dashed line marks it.
  • Nairal, 92, remembers being introduced to Vlen as his great-uncle. That puts Vlen two generations above Nairal, beside an unknown sibling who is Nairal's grandparent.
  • The Jocells come from "a cousin who left Vaca Goya to find work in the starport". Nobody has said which generation, so they hang off the family by a dashed line.
