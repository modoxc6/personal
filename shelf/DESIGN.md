# Design — The Shelf

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

The world is a video rental shop after closing: a dim aisle, worn oak shelving, jackets
stored edge-on, card-stock talkers clipped to the shelf lip. Every element on the page
is a fixture in that shop. If a new component has no counterpart in a rental shop,
find its counterpart before styling it.

## The one rule

**The archive's resting state is edge-on spines.** Face-out jackets are the exception,
reserved for what is newest. A change that turns the wall into a grid of posters has
left this design, however good it looks — the density and the physicality are the idea.

## Aisles

Two aisles share one shop and differ by exactly one token: the card stock.

| | TV aisle | Film aisle |
|---|---|---|
| `data-shelf` | `tv` | `film` |
| `--label` | `#e9d24b` acid yellow | `#c08bff` fluoro violet |
| `--label-ink` | `#241a06` | `#1d0b30` |

The card stock drives the lit aisle sign, every shelf-talker, rating pips, pressed
filter chips, the stock count, and the counter's tag stickers. Nothing else changes.
Adding a third aisle means adding a `data-shelf` block and an entry in `SHELVES`, not
a new palette.

## Fixtures (shared, never per-aisle)

| Token | Value | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `--carpet` | `#180f12` | oxblood shop floor; the page ground |
| `--board` / `--board-lit` / `--board-dark` | `#4a3423` / `#8a6540` / `#2a1c12` | shelf board, its lit front edge, its shadowed underside |
| `--divider` | `#16a89a` | teal plastic section dividers (the year tabs) |
| `--ink` / `--ink-dim` | `#f2e8da` / `#a08f7d` | shop lighting on paper |
| `--sign-off` | `#4b3a33` | an unlit aisle sign |
| `--hot` | `#e2482f` | the in-progress edge on a spine; nothing else |

Dark is not a preference here — it is closing time. Do not add a light theme.

## Type

- **Archivo 900** — aisle signs and the counter's title. Shop signage: heavy, condensed-feeling, all caps.
- **Archivo Narrow 600/700** — spine lettering, divider tabs, filter chips. Spines are condensed because they have to fit.
- **Courier Prime** — every piece of paperwork: shelf-talkers, the stock count, the counter's record rows, the result count. If it would have been typed or printed in the back office, it is Courier.

## Geometry

`--spine-w: 43px`, `--spine-h: 232px`, `--board-h: 38px` (30/168/30 under 640px).
Shelves wrap, and the board is painted by a `repeating-linear-gradient` on `.shelf`
with a cycle of `--spine-h + --board-h`, so every wrapped row gets its own floor.
Changing a spine dimension without changing the others breaks the board rhythm.

## Behaviours the world owns

- **Spine art** is a narrow vertical slice of the real jacket: the `<img>` renders at
  520px wide and the spine crops it. `object-fit: cover` shows a third of the poster
  and reads as a colour smear, not a shelf.
- **The bookend** closes every shelf, and the last spine leans into it at −3.5°.
- **Hover lifts a spine 16px out of the shelf**; nothing else moves.
- **Clicking takes it to the counter** — a panel rising from the bottom edge, never a
  centred modal. You carry a case to the counter.
- **Filtering shortens the shelf.** Non-matching spines leave; the boards and bookends
  re-flow. The count on the rail is the only running total.

## Data contract

`shows.json` and `movies.json` are generated by `build.mjs` from the Obsidian vault and
are never hand-edited. Per-aisle differences in filters, counter rows, search fields and
the stats line live in the `SHELVES` map in `index.html` — one object per aisle, no
branching in the render path.

Covers are resized to 340px wide into `covers/tv/` and `covers/film/`. Spines use
`loading="lazy"`; with 749 films on one page, eager loading is not optional to fix later.
Spine elements are memoised per entry (`s._el`) so filtering moves nodes instead of
rebuilding them and refetching every jacket.
